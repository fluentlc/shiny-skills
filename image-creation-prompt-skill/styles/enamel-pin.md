**Fill-in rules:**
- Metal outline color (gold / silver / rose gold) should match the character's or subject's palette and emotional tone
- Enamel fill should use 3–5 distinct colors, each clearly separated by the metal outline (authentic enamel pin construction)
- Background fill within the pin should echo the character's universe or emotional world
- Composition should be top-down product photography — the pin is centered on a clean neutral surface with precise studio lighting
- Subject pose should be iconic and immediately recognizable — the pin should work as a collectible at a glance
- Shadow gradients and linework should give the flat enamel a slight 3D dimensionality without breaking the "enamel" aesthetic
