**Sections:**

```

Font style: Varied hand-drawn typography, mixing script and bold capital letters

=== SCENE & LAYOUT ===
Composition: [choose one: winding roadmap journey across a parchment landscape / ascending staircase with milestone banners / radial map from a central hub]

=== MAIN CONTENT ===

[STEP_N]
Visual: [detailed hand-drawn diagram or scene representing the step: e.g., terminal window with code syntax, modular hexagonal stack, levitating gemstone] with [stick-figure developer / domain expert / abstract icon] as supporting illustration
Action: [what conceptual role this step plays in the overall narrative]
Label: "[step name]：[step description in Chinese]" in handwritten text within banners, boxes, or speech bubbles
[If not first step] Connection: [thick ribbon arrow in burnt orange / mustard yellow / sepia] flowing diagonally upward-right, edged with fine cross-hatching or starburst flourishes

=== VISUAL STYLE ===
Material: Ink pen outlines with visible texture, colored pencil and watercolor wash shading.
Render: 2D traditional illustration aesthetic, looks like a high-quality scan of a handmade journal page.
Colors: Muted, warm vintage palette (olive green, burnt orange, mustard yellow, muted blue, sepia tones, cream), low to medium saturation.

=== DECORATIVE ELEMENTS ===
Ornamental dividers, flourishes, filigree, small thematic icons (magnifying glass, lightbulbs, arrows, stars), cross-hatching shading, and dotted lines to fill negative space and connect elements.

=== MOOD ===
Retro academic, adventurous, informative, nostalgic, hand-crafted, highly detailed, studious.

Quality: High-quality scan of traditional illustration, rich textures, legible but artistic handwriting style.
```

**Fill-in rules:**
- Connection arrows should escalate in weight and flourish as steps increase in importance (thin sepia for step 1→2, thick mustard ribbon for step 2→3)
- Each step's visual metaphor should feel like an artifact or discovery (code as a scroll, protocol as an interlocking mechanism, skill as a radiant gemstone or compass)
- Background parchment texture should feel lived-in — add subtle stains, crease lines, or margin notes in the corners
