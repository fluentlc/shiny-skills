**Fill-in rules:**
- This style is designed for cultural tourism promotion, scenic spot posters, and traditional Chinese aesthetic visuals
- Landscape must evoke classical Chinese shanshui painting: layered mountains with atmospheric perspective (distant peaks fading into mist), water as a reflective or flowing element, and vegetation rendered in ink-wash style
- The human figure should be integrated into the landscape, not dominant — typically small in scale, wearing flowing Hanfu in pale colors (white, light blue, pale green)
- Main calligraphy text should be a poetic 5–7 character phrase that captures the location's essence; secondary text can be a 2–4 word subtitle
- Text placement should not obstruct the landscape's focal point; prefer top-center, bottom-right, or vertically along one edge
- Color palette must be restrained and natural: emerald greens, turquoise blues, soft whites, ink blacks, and subtle gold accents only for text
- Atmospheric effects should include mist, light rays through clouds, water reflections, and soft particle dust to enhance the ethereal mood
- If the topic is a specific location (e.g., 都江堰), include authentic geographical or architectural details (ancient irrigation system, Qingcheng mountain backdrop)
- Optional date/venue information should be placed discreetly at the bottom in small clean font, not competing with the calligraphy
