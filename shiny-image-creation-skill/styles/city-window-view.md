# Style Template: 城市窗外风景照 (City Window View Style)

**Input format:** `[character] in [location/scenario], 城市窗外风景照风格, [aspect_ratio]`

> ⚠️ This style generates a character-in-scene cinematic photograph. Input is a character + location/scenario, not an educational topic.

**Opening Line:**
```
Generate a photorealistic window view poster based on the following data:
location: [specific city or fictional setting], specific_view: [landmark or skyline description], Weather: [atmospheric weather condition], aspect_ratio: [aspect_ratio]
```

**Sections:**

```
| [character-specific cinematic quality tags]:
[character name] [seated/standing] [position relative to window — e.g., 静坐窗边, 靠窗站立], [activity — e.g., 阅读纸质书, 凝视窗外, 喝咖啡], [posture and expression details], [specific hand position and gesture], [what they are holding or interacting with], 窗外[describe the view: cityscape, architecture, weather, landmark silhouettes, light quality, color temperature], 整体氛围[mood in 10–20 characters: e.g., 寂静、空灵、略带存在主义忧郁]

[Character-specific appearance quality tags:]
[hair: color, style, light interaction], [eyes: color, expression, focus direction], [clothing: specific costume, fabric detail, color], [skin: tone, subsurface scattering, light reflection], [special effects: glow, translucency, bokeh on surroundings]

8k resolution, depth of field (blurred background), photorealistic photography, ultra-detailed, sharp focus, studio-quality lighting, cinematic color grading, soft shadows, film grain texture, [camera model] realism
```

**Fill-in rules:**
- The window is the compositional anchor: character occupies foreground, cityscape fills mid-to-background
- Weather and lighting are mood carriers: overcast for melancholy, golden hour for warmth, night for loneliness
- Character's activity should feel intimate and private, not posed (reading, gazing, writing)
- Window glass should show subtle reflections layering interior and exterior for depth
- Describe the city view with specific fictional or real landmarks to ground the scene
