# Example: 美式超级英雄漫画风格

## 用户输入

```
生成美式超级英雄漫画的图片，内容是从Function Call到MCP到SKILL的发展历程，16:9
```

## 生成的 Prompt

```
Generate a comic strip in the style of a Modern American Superhero Graphic Novel, 16:9, containing 3 panels, explaining 从Function Call到MCP到SKILL的发展历程.

=== TITLE ===
"从Function Call到MCP到SKILL的发展历程" in #FF3333 bold, blocky superhero comic font with 3D or distressed effects
Position: Top center or integrated into the first splash panel, highly dynamic

=== SCENE & LAYOUT ===
Background: Dynamic action backgrounds, speed lines, debris, or detailed cityscapes
Layout: 3 panels with dynamic composition (diagonal cuts, overlapping panels, or characters breaking panel borders)
Constraint: Visuals only contain the title, sound effects (SFX), and the comic panels. No extra floating text outside bubbles.

=== MAIN CONTENT ===
[PANEL_1] (large, top-left third)
Scene: A sleek, glowing blue digital interface shatters like glass as a lightning-bolt-shaped "FUNCTION CALL" icon bursts forward — code fragments flying outward, binary rain in background
Character(s): Abstract anthropomorphic CPU core with circuit-patterned armor, eyes pulsing with green light
Action & Expression: Leaping forward mid-air, fist extended, determined gaze, sparks trailing from fingertips
Dialogue (Speech Bubble): "执行！"
Sound Effects (Big Bold Text): "KRA-KOOM!"
Narration (Rectangular Box): "起点：函数调用——最基础、最直接的指令触发机制"

[PANEL_2] (medium, center)
Scene: The CPU core lands atop a massive, multi-layered neural network scaffold rising from a data ocean; glowing purple "MCP" glyphs orbit like satellites around a central command nexus
Character(s): Same CPU core now wearing a tactical HUD visor and linked via fiber-optic tendons to three AI agents (stylized as armored drones with holographic schematics)
Action & Expression: Crouching low, arms outstretched, synchronizing connections — face calm but intensely focused
Dialogue (Speech Bubble): "协同！编排！"
Sound Effects (Big Bold Text): "HUMMMMM—SYNC!"
Narration (Rectangular Box): "演进：MCP（Model Control Protocol）——实现多模型协同与流程编排的智能中枢"

[PANEL_3] (large, bottom-right two-thirds, bleeding across right edge)
Scene: The MCP nexus explodes upward into radiant golden light, reforming as a towering, radiant humanoid figure composed of interlocking gears, neural synapses, and skill-badge icons (e.g., "Code", "Reason", "Tool Use", "Plan"); behind it, a futuristic city skyline pulses with adaptive infrastructure
Character(s): SKILL Avatar — a luminous, gender-neutral hero clad in modular exo-armor engraved with micro-documentation, holding a glowing skill manifest scroll
Action & Expression: Standing tall, one foot on a crumbling "MCP Core" pedestal, raising scroll skyward — serene, authoritative, visionary
Dialogue (Speech Bubble): "自主！演化！"
Sound Effects (Big Bold Text): "SHIIIIINE—EVOLVE!"
Narration (Rectangular Box): "跃迁：SKILL——可注册、可组合、可演化的原子化智能能力单元，构建下一代AI操作系统"

=== SUPERHERO COMIC STYLE ===
Modern Western comic book aesthetic (Marvel/DC style). Bold, heavy black ink outlines with dramatic shading. Vibrant, saturated digital coloring with high contrast lighting. Usage of "Speed Lines" and "Impact Flashes" to convey motion. Characters often break the panel borders (bleed effect) to enhance depth. Visuals are gritty yet polished, emphasizing muscle definition, technology details, and destruction physics.

=== MOOD ===
Action-packed, intense, heroic, cinematic, high-energy, dramatic

Quality: High-quality comic book art, sharp details, dynamic perspective, professional coloring | 8k, masterpiece, best quality, ultra-detailed, cinematic lighting, intricate linework, vivid color grading, comic book cover grade
```
