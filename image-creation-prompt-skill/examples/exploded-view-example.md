# Example: 物品爆炸视图风格

## 用户输入

```
解析一下switch机器结构，16:9
```

## 生成的 Prompt

```
Technical exploded view diagram, 16:9, featuring switch机器结构.

=== COMPOSITION & PERSPECTIVE ===
View: Isometric perspective (30-degree angle)
Layout: Deconstructed assembly, all individual components floating in space (expanded view)
Alignment: Parts aligned in logical assembly order along the central axis, revealing internal structure
Depth: Clear separation between layers to show how they fit together

=== COMPONENTS ===
Subject: switch机器结构 broken down into key parts:

主板（含Tegra X1处理器、内存、eMMC存储芯片、电源管理IC）

Joy-Con控制器（左/右，含IMU惯性测量单元、HD震动马达、NFC模块、蓝牙天线）

主机机身外壳（上下壳体，含散热鳍片、导热硅脂层、金属屏蔽罩）

底座组件（含HDMI接口、USB-C供电接口、LAN网口、支架铰链结构）

可拆卸电池组（锂聚合物电芯、保护电路板、温度传感器）

LCD屏幕模组（7英寸IPS面板、触控IC、背光LED阵列、柔性排线）

Detail: High mechanical precision, distinct geometry for each part

=== LABELS & ANNOTATIONS ===
Style: Clean leader lines pointing to major components
Text: 中文 labels (e.g., "主板", "Joy-Con左控制器", "底座HDMI接口", "LCD屏幕模组")
Constraint: Professional engineering standard, legible sans-serif font

=== VISUAL STYLE ===
Aesthetic: Technical illustration style, similar to engineering blueprints or patent drawings
Lighting: Soft ambient occlusion shadows between floating parts to enhance depth perception
Background: Clean white

=== MOOD & QUALITY ===
Educational, industrial, precise, informative infographic aesthetic | 8k, ultra-detailed, technical masterpiece, sharp focus, engineering blueprint accuracy, labeled annotation clarity, isometric precision
```
