=== TEXT ELEMENTS ===

=== COMPOSITION ===
[composition_style] layout featuring a central flowing energy ribbon made of luminous particles, surrounded by interconnected nodes and holographic interfaces. Depth created through layered transparency effects and gradient lighting.

=== ATMOSPHERE ===
High-tech ambiance with immersive glow, ambient occlusion, and subtle motion blur to suggest speed and innovation. The overall mood is visionary and forward-thinking.

**Fill-in rules:**
- This style is designed for large-scale conference posters, tech summit promotions, and visionary brand visuals
- Main title should be 2–4 powerful Chinese characters (e.g., "创见·未来") that capture the event's spirit
- English title is a direct or poetic translation placed below the Chinese title in smaller, elegant font
- Tagline is a longer explanatory phrase (10–20 characters) that clarifies the event's focus or mission
- Color palette defaults to deep blue + golden-orange contrast; the blue provides the "tech" feel, the warm accent adds energy and aspiration
- The central energy ribbon is the visual anchor — describe it as flowing particles, light streams, or a luminous sine wave that draws the eye through the composition
- Interconnected nodes around the ribbon suggest networks, AI, or distributed systems — adjust the metaphor to match the topic
- Circuit patterns and data streams in the background should be subtle and low-contrast, not competing with the foreground text
- Motion blur on the light streaks should feel directional (left→right or bottom→top) to create a sense of forward momentum
- The overall mood must be "visionary" — expansive, optimistic, and technologically advanced
