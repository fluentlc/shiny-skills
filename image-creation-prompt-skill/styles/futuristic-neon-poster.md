**Fill-in rules:**
- This style is designed for product launch announcements, tech conference posters, and futuristic event promotions
- Main title should be the event name in Chinese (e.g., "产品新品发布会"); subtitle captures the core themes in short punchy keywords separated by · (e.g., "创新·发展·智造")
- Date display is the visual centerpiece: render it large, luminous, and surrounded by energy waves — it should feel like a countdown or milestone marker
- Date format should match the user's input style: "11.14" for month.day, "2025.11.14" for full date, etc.
- Color palette defaults to deep blue (#000033) → near-black (#000011) gradient; accept user-specified accent colors but keep the dark background
- The reflective platform under the date creates a sense of stage/podium — describe ripple patterns and soft reflections
- Additional elements should be minimal: a small geometric icon (diamond, star, hexagon) or a tiny logo, placed at a corner without competing with the title or date
- Particle effects should drift upward consistently, creating vertical motion and energy flow
- Composition must be strictly symmetrical and centered — this is a poster, not a free-form illustration
