# Style Template: 真实感手写笔记风格 (Handwritten Math Notes)

**Opening Line:**
```
Handwritten mathematical notes style, [aspect_ratio], topic [topic].
```

**Sections:**

```
=== MATHEMATICAL CONTENT ===
[main_formula_or_concept] written in blue ink with red highlights, including [derivation_steps] and [graphical_representation], on lined notebook paper.

=== LAYOUT AND STRUCTURE ===
Organized into sections with headings like "[section_title_1]", "[section_title_2]", and "[section_title_3]", featuring annotations such as "[annotation_1]" and [example_expression_or_diagram].

=== VISUAL DETAILS ===
Blue handwriting with red underlines and arrows, clean lines, slight shadows from paper texture, natural lighting, realistic paper creases and folds, high-resolution detail (8k), masterpiece quality.

=== STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE ===
Academic tone, educational context, clear legibility, precise notation, authentic classroom or study environment feel.

=== ADDITIONAL ELEMENTS ===
Includes a graph or diagram of [concept_name] versus [variable_name] with labeled axes, points, and key milestones or asymptotic behavior. Mentions [special_case] and convergence or evolution conditions.

Quality: Ultra-detailed, sharp focus, no noise, accurate symbols, professional academic presentation, masterpiece.
```

**Fill-in rules:**
- The topic should be translated into mathematical or diagrammatic notation whenever possible: use arrows (→), functions f(t), layered diagrams, or flow notation
- Section headings should reflect temporal or logical phases (e.g., "阶段一", "阶段二", "未来趋势") rather than generic labels
- Blue ink is used for primary content; red ink is reserved for highlights, corrections, underlines, and connecting arrows
- Annotations should feel like a real student's margin notes — concise, slightly informal, sometimes using quotes or emphasis
- The graph/diagram must plot a meaningful variable against time or abstraction level, with 3–5 labeled milestone points
- Paper texture details (creases, shadows, slight curl at the corner) should be explicitly described to reinforce realism
- Example expressions should be concrete and domain-appropriate (code snippets for CS, equations for math, circuit diagrams for engineering)
