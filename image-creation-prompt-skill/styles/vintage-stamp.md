**Fill-in rules:**
- The stamp occupies the center of the frame against a near-black background — the background is as important as the stamp itself
- Icons inside the stamp should be engraving-style (linocut): simple, high-contrast, no photorealism
- The progression of concepts (step 1 → step 2 → step 3) should be shown as vertically stacked icons connected by tapered arrows
- Use gold foil accents sparingly on borders and title — they should feel like embossed printing, not decoration
- Typography inside the stamp: Song-style Chinese for the main title, small serif English labels for each icon
- The overall palette is muted sepia with occasional metallic gold — no vivid colors
