**Fill-in rules:**
- The 9 panels should show variety: different expressions (happy, surprised, shy, determined, pouty, playful), different poses, and different decorative frames per panel
- Booth background signs should reference the character's series or universe in Japanese katakana
- Color palette should reflect the character's signature palette (hair color, costume color) combined with neon pink/purple photo booth lighting
- The hand holding the strip should be realistic and in foreground — creates the "just printed" photo strip aesthetic
- Each panel frame can have different kawaii decorations (hearts, stars, flowers, speech bubbles)
