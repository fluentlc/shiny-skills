**Fill-in rules:**
- Each step should use a distinct clay color to make stages visually distinguishable at a glance
- Objects should show realistic clay imperfections: fingerprint marks, slight surface irregularities, soft edges
- Connection arrows between steps should be soft and organic (curved, not sharp) to match the tactile clay aesthetic
- Final step can use a branching or radiating structure to convey breadth and maturity
